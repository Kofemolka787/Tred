## Signal collection and AI review

Version 0.4.3 keeps the selected market warm before and between analysis requests. The worker-owned collector is reused without a per-job page reload or source UUID reset. Manual selection prioritizes that instrument while the other eligible market charts continue loading. M1/M5/M15 history is prepared in the background with bounded pacing and lower priority than current analysis tasks. Explicit user closure pauses the collector until a new request. Collection never makes a paid model request.

Windows startup now opens a desktop status window with the saved AI model, an Open Bot button that copies the current verified public route, one-time AI setup, and graceful stop of its own backend/tunnel. Verified unchanged dependencies skip repeated installation. The connector can recover the user's existing authorized token through the fixed http://127.0.0.1:8790 endpoint without the obsolete public hostname or a new pairing code. Other local hosts and ports are rejected; rejected tokens preserve the saved route. The extension needs loopback host permission once. The Mini App still uses verified public HTTPS, with Telegram CloudStorage restoring the route across devices. Ephemeral Cloudflare addresses still need one paste into the bot when changed; this is not a claim of permanent hosting.

Early Active triggers retain a server-side entry contract containing the original source, twelve anchor candles, direction, zone and invalidation. A new closed bar during model review is permitted only inside the bounded lifetime and after fresh history, source, context, current price and anchor checks. Broken levels, altered anchors, expired contracts and departed zones remain rejected with specific reasons. Tracking revalidates the same contract. The actual model selects the original scenario; no automatic second paid review or random/rule-only fallback is introduced. Existing Strict and Active modes, explicit risk labels and 80-bar requirements remain.

The native chart collection introduced in 0.4.0 remains. The extension creates one inactive Pocket Option collector tab using the browser's existing login, registers its role before navigation, then requests chart histories with changeSymbol followed by subfor. Foreground tabs keep the passive/advanced-history path and do not receive generated chart changes. The collector tab has a visible TRED banner. No session, cookie or password is copied, replayed or sent to the backend.

Closing the collector pauses that job instead of creating an opening loop. A new user scan can resume it; analysis does not reload an existing collector. Collector-role catalogs have bounded precedence over foreground catalogs; source IDs remain explicit, and closed collector sources are explicitly invalidated, including after worker restart. The decoder now handles updateHistory, nested Socket.IO attachments, interleaved events, indexed bare responses, legacy OHLC columns and null-extended tick tuples. Identity and period require explicit fields or exact request correlation.

The received catalog retains every instrument, including OTC and non-currency categories. Open instruments must meet the selected expiry and payout filters (prefer 92%, floor 85%). Five independent base setup families cover Smart Money, EMA20/50 pullbacks, breakout/retests, range rebounds and false breakouts, with three additional early families in Active mode. Closed-bar calculations establish causal scenarios; LunaRoute or direct OpenAI compares them and chooses an exact symbol/direction/strategy tuple or declines. This is model-assisted analysis with deterministic validation, not model-weight training or measured win probabilities.

Short native history pages are joined using explicit older end bounds, within stored-history limits and the selected source clock; stale data is refreshed at the current time first. Partial compatible periods such as M2/M10 are completed instead of being replaced with empty preferred periods. Diagnostics show actual continuous bars and age separately from decoded response counts.

Chart review is separated from final entry validation. Full, continuous closed histories on two periods can enter AI review without simultaneous live ticks for every pair. The closed-bar reference is explicitly separate from the real quote. Incomplete pairs retain inventory and missing-data summaries. Partial collection can start review after a short grace period when a scenario exists; an empty initial shortlist keeps collecting until complete or deadline. Large catalogs use bounded batches and winner comparison, preserving all inventory rows and complete-chart facts.

After AI selection the collector focuses on the chosen pair. No direction or entry button is offered until a genuine quote at most 10 seconds old and the original scenario, price zone, payout, expiry and source are revalidated. Missing selected quotes get a bounded 12-second check, without another paid model call. Stable Smart Money anchors may survive a new closed bar only after full fresh validation; early Active setups can survive another bar only under their bounded revalidated original contract; other candle setups require the original bar. Source changes, invalidated structures and departed price zones cancel entry. No forced rule-only fallback or automatic paid retries.

The UI distinguishes catalog rows, complete charts reviewed and fresh quotes. Indicator facts and causal levels are calculated from 80–160 closed bars; the model receives a compact recent OHLC sample. Periods are seconds (300=M5, 900=M15), with trade durations separately in minutes. The selected strategy, explanation and weakness remain in the manual trade journal.

## Connector and update

Update the entire ZIP: server 0.4.3, the same extension card to connector 0.4.3, and the Mini App interface. Refresh Pocket Option once after extension update and restart the new server. The existing private database, provider key, chosen model and confirmed account profiles persist. An unchanged server route needs no new pairing code; a changed temporary tunnel address must be saved in the bot; the desktop connector recovers its local route automatically. See the Russian setup guide for exact Windows steps.

The connector recovers the worker's feed-to-tab mapping from actual live MAIN sockets after worker restart. Native incoming messages renew collection plans and wake the queue when background page timers are delayed. Read-only subfor/loadHistoryPeriod requests and collector-tab-only changeSymbol requests share one-per-400ms pacing, at most two pending replies and one request per instrument. Native chart initializations are serialized. Manual selection and the model-selected missing quote receive extra priority. No order, login or account mutation commands are generated by TRED.

Bad candle rows are excluded separately rather than rejecting an otherwise valid history packet. At least 80 recent continuous closed candles are still required, and gaps are never filled. History and quotes from different connections are isolated. Transient delivery retries retain original timestamps and are bounded. UTC or explicit Pocket UTC+2 conversion, source validation and final quote freshness checks remain; closed prices are never relabeled as live ticks. The popup shows collection state and sanitized diagnostics.

## Local setup and privacy

SETUP-LUNAROUTE.cmd fetches the authenticated model catalog and verifies a short paid structured Responses request before saving the key. SETUP-AI.cmd supports direct OpenAI. Keys are entered visibly in the local setup console as requested by the owner and stored only in the private user directory, scoped to authorized signed Telegram users. No key belongs in Telegram, the Mini App or this repository. The LunaRoute gateway is https://gw.lunaroute.com/v1; no Node.js/CLI login is required. ChatGPT subscription billing is separate from API billing. Failed setup preserves previous settings; transport, quota and invalid response failures have sanitized distinct messages.

The bounded Responses client accepts completed validated SSE results without waiting for connection EOF. Immutable evidence references are checked against source facts; fabricated, cross-pair, duplicate or partial output cannot become an entry. Provider changes discard in-flight results. Only market evidence is sent to the model; no broker credentials, account balances or owner identifiers.

Demo and real balances are read only. The same signed Telegram user can follow received quotes on another device after independently opening a trade, then report win/loss/draw after expiry. Manual outcomes remain separate from broker balances. The computer, terminal and server must stay running for this local deployment.

## Verification and limits

Automated tests cover strategy recognition in both directions on 5/10/15-minute durations, source/time/zone guards, mobile UI, manual tracking, user isolation, worker recovery and preserved balances. A new 23-pair fixture returns no advanced OHLC but supplies native nested updateHistory data: the actual collector, observer, worker, HTTP backend and Responses client reach a validated decision on all three durations. Browser-API fixtures check singleton tab creation, role registration before navigation, foreground isolation, explicit-close behavior and restart cleanup. Existing zero-tick, short-page and cold-catalog cases remain. These are controlled fixtures, not native broker latency measurements.

The installed owner's Opera GX extension, live Pocket stream and actual LunaRoute replies still require post-installation verification. Managed Chromium prevents native unpacked-extension loading in this test environment; no policy bypass is used. No claim of profitability, trained weights, real exchange order flow for OTC, or guaranteed signals is made. Empty or invalid data and absent confirmed setups remain explicit waits rather than invented entries. No bot tokens, broker sessions, passwords, private databases or owner IDs are packaged.
